# Requirement Gathering Questionnaire

**Project:** Hostel Mess Feedback, Menu and Rebate Management System  
**Method:** Structured questionnaire circulated online  
**Target respondents:** Resident students of Hostel D (Neeram Hall), TIET, Patiala  
**Sample size aimed for:** 40–50 responses  
**Instrument:** Google Forms  
**Form link:** [Hostel Mess Experience Survey](https://forms.gle/zP4XDS6Ryxd4Ej2c7)  
**Date of circulation:** 8 September 2026  

## Purpose

The questionnaire was designed to verify from the students who actually use the mess, the three problems identified in Section 3. Each question is mapped to one of those problems so that the responses can be used as evidence when stating the functional requirements of the proposed system.

## Note on Method

A questionnaire was chosen over interviews because the problems concern the routine experience of a large number of students rather than the detailed working of any one role and a sample of this size gives a more reliable picture of how widespread each problem is. Informal discussion with members of the mess committee was used alongside the survey to understand the administrative side of the process which students do not observe directly.
